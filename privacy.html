Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

Welcome to FreeToolsWorld. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

FreeToolsWorld does not require users to create an account. We do not intentionally collect or store files or text that users enter into our tools on our own servers.

Most tools are designed to process information directly in your browser. However, some features may use external services, as explained below.

2. How Our Tools Work

Image Compressor, Age Calculator, Word Counter, Password Generator, Text Case Converter, URL Encoder/Decoder and Calculator are designed to work in your browser.

Our QR Code Generator uses an external QR code service. Text or URLs entered into that tool may be sent to that service to generate a QR code.

Image to PDF and PDF to Image tools use browser-based processing libraries. Please avoid uploading sensitive documents to any online tool unless you understand how the tool works.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services and libraries, including:

  • GitHub Pages for website hosting.
  • cdnjs for loading software libraries.
  • QRServer API for QR code generation.

These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review the policies of those services.

4. Cookies

FreeToolsWorld does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or user tracking in its current implementation. However, third-party services or hosting providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

5. Data Security

We aim to keep our website simple and safe. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless necessary.

6. Children's Privacy

FreeToolsWorld is a general-purpose website. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted to us, please contact the website owner.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or services change. Any updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the website owner using the contact information provided on the website, when available.
